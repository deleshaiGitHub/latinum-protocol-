Developer Whitepaper: The Latinum Protocol (TLP) v1.0
Technical Specification for an Omnichain Treasury-Backed Synthetic Asset
1. System Overview
The Latinum Protocol is an omnichain primitive consisting of a Fixed-Rate Minting Press, a Global Treasury (The Continuum), and a Native Liquidity Distribution Engine. It utilizes LayerZero OFT-V2 for unified token supply and Chainlink CCIP for cross-chain state synchronization.
2. Tokenomics Architecture
Asset Name: Gold-Pressed Latinum ($GPL)
Standard: Omnichain Fungible Token (OFT-V2)
Total Hard Cap: 500,000,000,000 (500B)
Genesis Allocation: 100% held in the LatinumPress smart contract.
Fractional Reserve: 0.25% of every mint is diverted to the NagusInsuranceVault.
3. The "Press" Mechanism (Primary Market)
The LatinumPress is a multi-chain factory contract.
Fixed Mint Rate: 1.00 USDC = 1,000,000 GPL (Configurable by $FED Governance).
The Lock-Up: Minted 
 is non-transferable and held in a GestationVault until the Global Threshold Event is triggered.
Global Threshold: $1,000,000 USD (Aggregate across all chains).
Inflow Logic:
User deposits USDC on Chain A.
LatinumPress calls CrossChainStateSync to update the global mint counter.
GPL is minted to the user’s address but remains Locked.
4. The "Great Exchange" (Native Omnichain DEX)
Post-threshold, the protocol initializes the secondary market.
Liquidity Ratio: 50% of Treasury Stablecoins are paired with 50B 
 to seed the initial LP.
The Swap Engine: An intent-based aggregator (Squid/Li.Fi SDK wrapper) allowing "Any-Asset-to-GPL" swaps.
Real Yield Loop: 100% of trading fees generated by the LP are streamed to users with Locked GPL in the GestationVault.
5. Technical Stack & Standards
Smart Contracts: Solidity 0.8.24+ (Foundry/Hardhat).
Interoperability: LayerZero V2 (OFT & OApp).
Frontend: Next.js 14+, Tailwind CSS, Shadcn/UI.
Wallet Integration: WalletConnect v3 / Account Abstraction (EIP-4337) for gasless 
 trades.
Indexing: The Graph (Subgraphs) for real-time Treasury analytics.
6. Security Invariants (AI Audit Rules)
Mint Cap Enforcement: The total supply of 
 across all chains must NEVER exceed 500B.
Backing Integrity: The Treasury must always maintain a minimum of 
GPL$ in circulation.
The "Rule #1" Fee: Redemption from the Treasury must always incur a 5% "Exit Tax" to prevent bank runs and increase the floor price for remaining holders.
7. The ".25" Nagus Reserve Logic
The NagusInsuranceVault acts as a protocol-owned safety buffer. It is used for:
Arbitrage Rebalancing: Maintaining price parity across chains.
Impermanent Loss Mitigation: Subsidizing the main DEX pools during high volatility.
De-peg Protection: Backstopping the treasury if a whitelisted stablecoin loses its peg.
